# Service icon source and usage notes

The three Simple Icons files are sourced from commit d0b3c2d7153794b912913746fc0498a5d3211727. The exact upstream license and disclaimer are retained as SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md and SIMPLE-ICONS-DISCLAIMER.md. The upstream project uses CC0-1.0, which does not grant trademark rights. Original SVG path data is retained in source/; display assets add the corresponding brand color only.

The four telecom providers use publicly available official artwork (corporate website icons or the official app artwork published by the provider on the Apple App Store). Their availability does not establish an open-source license. The project records source URLs and modifications in services.sources.json; brand copyright and trademark rights remain with their owners. These assets identify user-selected accounts and do not indicate sponsorship, partnership, payment acceptance, or authorization from a provider.

- China Mobile: extracted the existing symbol group from the official combined SVG; paths and blue/green colors are preserved. source/ retains the original lockup.
- China Unicom: official 192×192 PNG with its original white background. The source SVG lockup embeds a raster image and is deliberately not advertised as vector artwork.
- China Telecom: official 100×100 PNG with its original blue background, suitable for a 32pt icon at 3×. Avoid scaling this file for larger illustrations.
- China Broadnet: official 512×512 app icon, including its original 5G corner badge and white background. The corporate website lockup is retained in source/ as a reference but is not the display icon.

QQ Wallet uses the QQ brand symbol, and WeChat Balance uses the WeChat brand symbol. Neither is labeled as a distinct official wallet logo. Phone-credit templates should represent the user's prepaid balance, not free data allowance, promotional credit, or outstanding phone bills.
